HOKSTATS.GG

About HoKStats.gg

A living Honor of Kings database, utility and community hub.

PURPOSE

Built around accurate information.

HoKStats.gg is designed to combine a structured game database with practical tools, historical information, guides and community features.

The project prioritizes verified information instead of filling missing data with guesses.

DATA

Source hierarchy

  1. Official live data
  2. Official announcements
  3. Verified historical data
INDEPENDENT

Project status

HoKStats.gg is an independent project and is not presented as an official Honor of Kings site.

DATA HANDLING

Live, test and unconfirmed data stay separate.

Test-server information is not silently treated as live data. Leaks and rumors are separated from the live database and are clearly labeled.

LANGUAGES

International first

The interface is designed to support English, Vietnamese, Chinese, French, Malay, Indonesian, Filipino, Japanese and Spanish.

COMMUNITY

Built for players

Community Q&A, guides, builds and discussion are designed to complement the structured database.

HIGH

MEDIUM

LOW

Never enter live DB
